Questions & answers

What you may want to know.

Property location, current availability, room arrangements, and housing rates are shared privately with appropriate applicants.

What is Tailored Care Living?

Tailored Care Living provides furnished independent shared housing for qualified adults seeking a clean, safe, affordable place to live.

Is this assisted living?

No. We provide independent housing and do not provide nursing care, personal-care assistance, medication administration, or daily supervision.

Do residents need to be independent?

Yes. Residents must be able to manage their own daily living needs without hands-on assistance.

Are meals provided?

No. Residents are responsible for purchasing and preparing their own food.

Who may apply?

Returning citizens, veterans, older adults, and other qualified independent adults are welcome to apply. Every application is reviewed individually.

Where is the home located?

For resident privacy and security, property information is provided to appropriate applicants during the application process.

How much does it cost?

Current housing rates and applicable payment details are discussed privately during the application process.

Will I have my own room?

Room arrangements and current availability are discussed during the application process.

What happens after I apply?

Tailored Care Living reviews your application and contacts appropriate applicants to discuss next steps. Applying does not guarantee acceptance or current availability.
