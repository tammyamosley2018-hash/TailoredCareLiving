# Client content needed

The Version 1 site is intentionally complete without these details. Add them before launch where available:

- Business email
- Business phone
- Application recipient email (`APPLICATION_RECIPIENT_EMAIL`)
- Owner/founder name and title
- Final legal business contact information
- Any final house rules intended for publication
- Any additional populations the client wants specifically named

Contact methods remain hidden until supplied. Property location, rates, availability, room arrangements, and detailed eligibility are intentionally discussed privately during the application process.
